Delhi Metro crowd FAQ

How crowded is the Delhi Metro right now?

MetroCrowd estimates crowd levels for every Delhi Metro station from historical ridership patterns, time of day and live community reports, and shows a 4-hour forecast.

Which Delhi Metro coach is least crowded?

The Coach Finder suggests the least crowded coach for your station, line and direction based on exit-gate positions and crowd patterns.

Is MetroCrowd free?

Yes. MetroCrowd is free to use without an account. Signing in lets you sync reports and earn reward points for completed trips, crowd reports and referrals.

Is MetroCrowd official DMRC data?

No. MetroCrowd is an independent app and is not affiliated with DMRC. Crowd levels are estimates.
